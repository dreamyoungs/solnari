# Solnari Community Code of Conduct

Solnari is built in public so that people can learn, review, and improve it together. We want the
project's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other community spaces to remain safe and
constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code with evidence; do not attack people.
- Welcome questions and contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Acknowledge tradeoffs and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private infrastructure, credentials, customer data, and personal information.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about project scope, safety, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or sexualized attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private database details, production data, or confidential reports.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith safety or conduct concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary project disagreements, contact the maintainers through a GitHub issue or pull request.
For sensitive conduct reports, use the private security-advisory channel linked from
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and mark the report as a community-safety concern. Do not disclose private
details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when needed. They will consider context, impact, intent,
prior behavior, and the safety of affected people, and will keep private reports confidential to the
extent reasonably possible.

This code applies whenever someone represents Solnari or participates in a project-managed space.
